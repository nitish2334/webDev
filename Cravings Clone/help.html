Frequently Asked Questions

You can track your order in real-time through the Cravings app or website. Go to "My Orders" section and click on the active order to see live tracking with your rider's location and estimated delivery time.

If you're not satisfied with your order, you can request a refund through the app. Go to "My Orders", select the order, and tap "Report Issue". Our support team will review and process your refund within 5-7 business days.

Typical delivery time is 30-45 minutes from the time you place your order. However, this may vary depending on your location, traffic conditions, and restaurant preparation time. You'll see the estimated delivery time when you confirm your order.

We accept multiple payment methods including credit/debit cards, digital wallets, and cash on delivery. You can choose your preferred payment method at checkout. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Yes, your personal and payment information is protected using industry-standard encryption and security protocols. We never share your information with third parties without your consent. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.

After your order is delivered, you'll receive a notification to rate the restaurant and delivery experience. You can provide ratings and detailed reviews to help other users make informed decisions.

Yes! We regularly offer discounts and promotions. Check the "Offers" section in the app for current deals. You can also apply promo codes during checkout if you have one.

You can contact our support team through the app's "Help" section, or email us at support@cravings.com. We also offer phone support during business hours. Response time is typically within 24 hours.
